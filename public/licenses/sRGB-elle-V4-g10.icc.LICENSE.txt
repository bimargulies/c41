sRGB-elle-V4-g10.icc
====================

A linear-gamma (gamma 1.0) ICC v4 RGB profile with sRGB primaries and the
D65 white point. C41 tools bundles it as the linear source space for the
"Correct gamma for raw scans" option (Assign Profile, then Convert to
Profile to the working RGB space).

The file is included UNMODIFIED, exactly as published.
